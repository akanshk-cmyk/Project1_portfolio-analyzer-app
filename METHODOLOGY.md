Average-cost analytics use Decimal internally. Buy fees increase basis; sell fees reduce sale proceeds. This is analytics, not tax basis. Realized gain or loss is not a tax figure. USD equities and ETFs only; no short sales.

**Total invested** is gross buy consideration plus buy fees. **Sale proceeds** are net of sell fees. **Net invested** is total invested minus sale proceeds. **Total gain** is market value plus sale proceeds minus total invested, equal to unrealized plus realized gain. Dividends = 0 in v1. Cumulative price return divides total gain by total invested, never net invested. Taxes are excluded; trade fees are included.

**Prices and splits:** yfinance `Close` with `auto_adjust=False` is split-adjusted, but not dividend-adjusted. Holdings replay split events before trades on the split date. Historical quantities are expressed in current split-adjusted units to match historical Close. Enter original trade-time shares and prices, not broker-restated split-adjusted trades. Non-trading-day trades use the next close for benchmark purchases and initial historical valuation. Prices are delayed and cached for 15 minutes; the common last-close date is shown. A stale badge appears after four calendar days. User-entered prices (zero allowed) affect current metrics only and are tagged.

**XIRR:** dated buy outflows, net sale inflows and terminal market value at common last market close; Actual/365. Fully sold portfolios use realized flows only. A bracketed solver scans log(1+r) and takes the detected root nearest zero. Search bounds near -100% through 1,000%. Same-day-only flows and complete losses without positive flows have no finite XIRR. Closely spaced/tangent roots may not be detected. Under one year, cumulative price return is the headline. Chart windows filter full-ledger values, not subperiod return calculations.

**Benchmark:** SPY by default, with QQQ, DIA and IWM available. Identical dated flows are applied to dividend-unadjusted Close. Sales requiring negative benchmark units make comparison unavailable. Comparisons need 90 days. Allocation is by holding, not look-through; no ETF-overlap, TWR or risk-adjusted statistics in v1.

**Corporate actions:** same-day trades use time (HH:MM:SS) if present, otherwise file order. Unknown tickers, unverified/non-USD currency and oversells block. Symbol redirection and unexplained price gaps above 60% block as possible corporate actions. These heuristics cannot comprehensively detect mergers, spinoffs, cash-in-lieu or ticker changes. Reconcile shares with your broker and correct unsupported events before relying on results. Transfers and dividend reinvestment are unsupported. An RSU vest may be represented as a BUY at fair value for analytics, not taxes.

**AI and privacy:** Groq selects existing fact IDs and methodology explanation IDs. Python renders the numbers. A strict verifier rejects unknown selections, retries once, then shows deterministic key stats. This limits conversational flexibility. Tools are read-only; maximum four calls per answer, 20 requests and 20,000 accounted tokens per session. Questions are independent; visible history is not sent. Account/notes columns are discarded. Only public market data is shared in the cache; portfolio state is session-only. Enabling AI sends aggregated holdings, metrics and your question to Groq. The operator must verify provider retention and no-training terms separately. Do not include personal identifiers in chat. CSV exports escape formula-like text cells.

**Release status:** local implementation, not cleared for public launch. yfinance is an unofficial personal/research data source. Human spreadsheet reconciliation, independent financial review, complete AI evaluations, privacy/security/legal review and licensed production data remain launch requirements. See GOVERNANCE.md.
